[LinkDev](https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/linkdev). The original Git history
is preserved in this repository.

GPL-3.0-or-later
[`actremotelink_pin_notify.c`](https://github.com/francoataffarel/ActRemoteLink/blob/main/actremotelink_pin_notify.c)

The pairing approach was informed by these open-source projects:

- [LinkDev](https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/linkdev)
